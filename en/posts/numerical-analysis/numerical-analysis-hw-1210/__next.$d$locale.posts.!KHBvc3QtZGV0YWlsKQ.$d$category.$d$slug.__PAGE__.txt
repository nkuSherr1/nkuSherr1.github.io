**Problem 1 (5.10).**

证明方程

$$
f(x) = x^3 - 6x - 12 = 0  在区间 [2, 5] 内有唯一实根  p ，并对任意的初始值  x_0 \in [2, 5] ，Newton 序列都收敛于  p 。

**证明.**

令  f(x) = x^3 - 6x - 12 ，则  f'(x) = 3x^2 - 6 ， f''(x) = 6x  为  f(x)  在 [2, 5] 上的二阶导数。

1.  f(2) \cdot f(5) = (8 - 12 - 12)(125 - 30 - 12) < 0 ；

2.  f'(x) = 3x^2 - 6 \geq 3 \cdot 2^2 - 6 = 6 > 0 ；

3.
$$

\left| \frac{f(2)}{f'(2)} \right| = \left| \frac{8 - 12 - 12}{3 \cdot 2^2 - 6} \right| = \frac{8}{3} < 3 = 5 - 2,
	\left| \frac{f(5)}{f'(5)} \right| = \left| \frac{125 - 30 - 12}{3 \cdot 5^2 - 6} \right| = \frac{83}{69} < 3 = 5 - 2 .

故由 ***第二版定理 5.7*** 知原命题成立。

∎

**Problem 2 (5.14).**

对  f(x) = 0  的 Newton 法，证明：

\lim_{n \to \infty} \frac{x_n - x_{n-1}}{(x_{n-1} - x_{n-2})^2} = -\frac{f''(x^*)}{2f'(x^*)}.

$$
**证明.**

设 x^* 是方程 f(x)=0 的最优解，且 f(x) 具有二阶或二阶以上的连续导数，满足 f'(x^*) \neq 0。牛顿法得到的迭代序列 \{x_k\} 收敛到 x^*。

牛顿法的迭代公式为：
$$

x_{k+1} = x_{k} - \frac{f(x_{k})}{f'(x_{k})}

$$
对于相邻的两步迭代 x_{n-2} \to x_{n-1} 和 x_{n-1} \to x_n，我们有：
$$

x_{n-1} - x_{n-2} = - \frac{f(x_{n-2})}{f'(x_{n-2})}

$$
\frac{f(x_{n-2})}{f'(x_{n-2})} = x_{n-2} - x_{n-1}
$$

以及：

$$
x_{n} - x_{n-1} = - \frac{f(x_{n-1})}{f'(x_{n-1})}
$$

我们对 $f(x_{n-1})$ 在 $x_{n-2}$ 处进行泰勒展开。令 $h = x_{n-1} - x_{n-2} = - \frac{f(x_{n-2})}{f'(x_{n-2})}$。

$$
f(x_{n-1}) = f(x_{n-2} + h) = f(x_{n-2}) + h f'(x_{n-2}) + \frac{h^2}{2} f''(x_{n-2}) + O(h^3)
$$

代入 $h = - \frac{f(x_{n-2})}{f'(x_{n-2})}$：

$$
\begin{aligned}
f(x_{n-1}) &= f(x_{n-2}) + \left(- \frac{f(x_{n-2})}{f'(x_{n-2})}\right) f'(x_{n-2}) + \frac{1}{2} \left(- \frac{f(x_{n-2})}{f'(x_{n-2})}\right)^2 f''(x_{n-2}) + O\left(\left(\frac{f(x_{n-2})}{f'(x_{n-2})}\right)^3\right) \\
&= f(x_{n-2}) - f(x_{n-2}) + \frac{1}{2} \frac{(f(x_{n-2}))^2}{(f'(x_{n-2}))^2} f''(x_{n-2}) + O\left(\left(\frac{f(x_{n-2})}{f'(x_{n-2})}\right)^3\right) \\
&= \frac{1}{2} \left( \frac{f(x_{n-2})}{f'(x_{n-2})} \right)^2 f''(x_{n-2}) + O\left(\left(\frac{f(x_{n-2})}{f'(x_{n-2})}\right)^3\right)
\end{aligned}
$$

由牛顿法公式和泰勒展开结果可得：

$$
\begin{aligned}
\lim_{n \to \infty} \frac{x_{n} - x_{n-1}}{(x_{n-1} - x_{n-2})^2} &= \lim_{n \to \infty} \frac{- \frac{f(x_{n-1})}{f'(x_{n-1})}}{ \left( \frac{f(x_{n-2})}{f'(x_{n-2})} \right)^2 } \\
&= \lim_{n \to \infty} \frac{- \frac{1}{f'(x_{n-1})} \left[ \frac{1}{2} \left( \frac{f(x_{n-2})}{f'(x_{n-2})} \right)^2 f''(x_{n-2}) + O \left( \left( \frac{f(x_{n-2})}{f'(x_{n-2})} \right)^3 \right) \right]}{ \left( \frac{f(x_{n-2})}{f'(x_{n-2})} \right)^2 } \\
&= \lim_{n \to \infty} \left( - \frac{1}{f'(x_{n-1})} \right) \left[ \frac{1}{2} f''(x_{n-2}) + \frac{ O \left( \left( \frac{f(x_{n-2})}{f'(x_{n-2})} \right)^3 \right) }{ \left( \frac{f(x_{n-2})}{f'(x_{n-2})} \right)^2 } \right] \\
&= \lim_{n \to \infty} \left( - \frac{1}{f'(x_{n-1})} \right) \left[ \frac{1}{2} f''(x_{n-2}) + O \left( \frac{f(x_{n-2})}{f'(x_{n-2})} \right) \right]
\end{aligned}
$$

由于 $x_n \to x^*$，当 $n \to \infty$ 时，我们有：

- $x_{n-1} \to x^*$，故 $f'(x_{n-1}) \to f'(x^*)$

- $x_{n-2} \to x^*$，故 $f''(x_{n-2}) \to f''(x^*)$

- $f(x_{n-2}) \to f(x^*) = 0$，故 $O \left( \frac{f(x_{n-2})}{f'(x_{n-2})} \right) \to O(0) = 0$

代入极限值：

$$
= - \frac{1}{f'(x^*)} \left( \frac{1}{2} f''(x^*) + 0 \right)

= - \frac{f''(x^*)}{2f'(x^*)}
$$

故：

$$
\lim_{n \to \infty} \frac{x_n - x_{n-1}}{(x_{n-1} - x_{n-2})^2} = -\frac{f''(x^*)}{2f'(x^*)}.
$$

∎
